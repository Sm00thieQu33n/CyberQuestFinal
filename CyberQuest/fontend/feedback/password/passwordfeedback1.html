Why is sharing a password unsafe?

Passwords should never be shared with coworkers or other people, even if you trust them or they claim to need access quickly.

Sharing your password can give another person access to your account and any information or systems connected to it. It also makes it difficult to determine who actually used the account.

If someone is having trouble accessing their account, they should use the organization's approved account recovery process or contact IT support.

What should you remember?

  • Never share your password with another person.
  • Do not let someone else use your account.
  • Use the appropriate account recovery process.
  • Contact IT support when additional assistance is needed.
