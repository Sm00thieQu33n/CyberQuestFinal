Why was this link suspicious?

The suspicious link was:

https://maryville-student-login.com

The important part of a website address is the actual domain. In this example, the domain is maryville-student-login.com. It is not the official maryville.edu domain.

A suspicious website may use words associated with a legitimate organization in an attempt to appear trustworthy. Always check the actual domain before entering your username, password, or other information.

What should you look for?

  • Check the actual domain name.
  • Be cautious of domains that imitate a legitimate organization.
  • Look for unusual words or extra domains in the address.
  • Do not assume a link is legitimate just because it contains the organization's name.
