Why was this the safest response?

An unattended and unlocked computer can expose sensitive information to people who are not authorized to access it. Noticing and reporting the situation can help protect the information and the organization.

You should avoid accessing, photographing, or changing information on someone else's computer. Instead, alert the coworker or the appropriate staff or security person so they can handle the situation.

What should you remember?

  • Do not ignore an unattended and unlocked computer.
  • Do not photograph or access someone else's information.
  • Report suspicious physical security situations to the appropriate person.
  • Protect sensitive information from unauthorized access.
