Why is tailgating a security risk?

Tailgating occurs when someone enters a restricted area by following an authorized person through a secured door without using their own credentials.

Even if the person appears to be an employee or claims to have forgotten their badge, you should not assume they are authorized to enter. Allowing unauthorized people into a secured area can put employees, equipment, and sensitive information at risk.

Follow your organization's access-control procedures. Politely direct the person to use their own badge or contact the appropriate staff member to verify their access.

What should you remember?

  • Do not let unknown people follow you into restricted areas.
  • Do not assume someone is authorized based on appearance.
  • Never lend your badge or credentials to another person.
  • Follow workplace access-control procedures.
  • Report suspicious access attempts when appropriate.
